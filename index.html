Amazon Firestick Setup

Fire TV Cube, Stick 4K & Lite models
Requirement: Fire OS 6.0+, Active internet connection, and Amazon remote.
1
Install Downloader App Go to the Amazon Appstore on your Firestick home screen, search for "Downloader" by AFTVnews, and click Get/Install.
2
Enable Unknown Sources Go to Settings > My Fire TV > Developer Options (if hidden, click About > Fire TV Stick 7 times). Turn on "Install Unknown Apps" for Downloader.
3
Enter Downloader Code Open Downloader, type shortcode 94821 in the URL box, and click Go to download and install BritTV.
Code: 94821

Xiaomi TV Stick Setup

Xiaomi TV Stick 4K & Android TV Dongles
Requirement: Android TV 11+, Google Play Store access.
1
Unlock Developer Options Go to Settings > Device Preferences > About > Scroll down to Build, and press OK 7 times until it confirms developer mode is active.
2
Install Downloader from Play Store Search for "Downloader" in the Google Play Store on your Xiaomi stick and install it.
3
Enter Downloader Code Open Downloader, allow storage permissions, enter shortcode 94822, and install the Xiaomi edition APK.
Code: 94822
